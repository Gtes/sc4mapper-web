Help

Start

  • Drop a 16-bit grayscale PNG or an SC4M file, or click the drop zone to choose one.
  • PNG size must be tiles × 64 + 1 (example: 1025×1025 → 16×16 tiles).
  • Cities are auto-gridded from a PNG. An SC4M file keeps the city layout it was saved with.
  • Name the region, then Download ZIP into Documents/SimCity 4/Regions/.
  • Unsaved work is drafted in this browser. Reload to continue.

Navigate

  • Wheel zooms toward the pointer. + / − / Fit in the map toolbar.
  • Space-drag or middle-drag pans.
  • Z-click zooms in. Alt+Z-click zooms out.

Cities

  • Small / Medium / Big place a city on click. Overlapping tiles are replaced.
  • Erase city removes the city under the click from the config.
  • Cities overlay shows borders on the map.
  • Revert config restores the auto-grid without changing terrain.

Draw

  • Paints height: Water (below the water line), Green, or a custom colour.
  • Sample map (or Alt-click in Draw) picks a nearby colour from the terrain.
  • Brush size and softness apply while you drag.

Stamp

  • Alt-click the map to pick source terrain (dashed ring).
  • Drag to clone that height and grain elsewhere.
  • Alt-click again to pick a new source. Size and softness match Draw.

Share

  • Download ZIP is the playable region: unzip into Documents/SimCity 4/Regions/.
  • Download SC4M is a share pack (height + city grid). It is not playable in-game by itself.
  • Drop an SC4M to open it, edit, then ZIP when you want to play, or SC4M again to share.

History

  • Undo / Redo: Ctrl+Z and Ctrl+Shift+Z (⌘Z / ⌘⇧Z on Mac).
  • Reset all clears the map, undo stack, and the saved draft after confirmation.

About

Browser port of SC4Mapper: 16-bit PNG and SC4M import, auto city grid, terrain palette, QFS / DBPF save, region ZIP, and SC4M export. Unofficial fan tool, not affiliated with EA or Maxis.
